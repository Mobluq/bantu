# Tour Studios — working notes for TikTok

Source: every page of https://www.tourstudio.tv, read on 11 November 2026.
These notes come from the website only. No footage has been reviewed yet.

## Who it is

- Tour Studios is the media and creative arm of Tour (thetourafrica.com), "the discovery platform for Lagos". Tagline: "Discover. Plan. Go."
- It makes films, editorial and photography about the people, places, communities and ideas shaping Lagos, for Tour itself and for brands, spaces and events.
- Positioning line: "We don't simply make promotional content. We find the story inside the place, person, event or community, and build the work around that."
- Channels today: Instagram (@officialtourng), YouTube (@officialtourng), Substack (tourafrica.substack.com). Contact: hello@tourstudio.tv.
- **No TikTok is linked anywhere on the site.** TikTok would be a new channel, not an existing one.
- A shop ("the Tour House": tees, caps) is announced as opening soon.

## Brand identity on the site

- The whole site is styled as a TV station: channels (CH 01–07), a programme guide by month, "On air", "Please stand by", call sheets, camera numbers, contact-sheet frame numbers.
- Voice: plain, confident, local. British spelling. Short declarative lines. Lagos is always the subject; the brand rarely talks about itself.
- Four things it looks for in every story: People, Places, Communities, Experiences.

## First read on TikTok (my interpretation, not from the site)

- The formats already map to TikTok series. "Guide to Lagos" is the most native: one person, a handful of named places, a clear question as the hook.
- The site's own hook lines are already written like TikTok openers ("You've seen the red building on Ademola Street. This is what's inside.").
- Most films run 1–4.5 minutes. Each should yield several 15–45 second cuts: one hook cut, one best-quote cut, one place-list cut.
- The editorial is an untapped source: quotes from the interviews can become text-led or voice-led clips over existing b-roll.
- The TV-station identity (channel bugs, "On air", programme guide) is a ready-made visual system for captions, lower thirds and end cards.
