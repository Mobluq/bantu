# Tour Studios on TikTok — editor's style guide

Built on 11 November 2026 from three things Folu supplied:
the strategy PDF ("Tour Studios on TikTok: the strategy"), five reference TikToks, and the call that Tayo's Guide to Lagos is the best film so far.
Where the sources disagree it is flagged under "Open conflicts"; nothing there is settled yet.

## 1. The rules (from the strategy PDF)

**Formats**

| Format | Length | Per week | Day | Signal it chases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| This Weekend | 30–45 s | 1 | Thursday | Saves, shares, search |
| At the Door | 20–45 s | 2 | Monday, Friday | Comments, shares |
| Tour With… | 60–120 s | 1 | Tuesday | Watch time, follows |
| The Series | 60–120 s | 1 when a season runs | Wednesday | Follows, completion |
| Same-night cut | 15–30 s | when it happens | Wed slot or weekend | — |

**First two seconds**
- Open on the best moment in the piece, even if it is from the middle. A person mid-sentence beats a wide shot.
- A title on screen from frame one that says what this is.
- No logo sting, no black frame, no "hey guys". Sound starts on frame one.

**Captions**
- Burned-in subtitles on every spoken word, proofread. Pidgin stays as spoken; Yoruba and Igbo get an English line.
- One typeface: the Tour Studios house sans, semibold, sentence case, white with a soft shadow. Never TikTok's default text.
- Two lines at most, lower middle, clear of the bottom fifth and the right-hand button strip.
- Calm broadcast subtitles. No word-by-word pop-ups, no emoji in frame.

**TV-station look, lightly**
- Small channel bug top-left, below the app's top bar: Tour Studios mark plus format name. Same place every video.
- Lower-third name strap for anyone on screen.
- One-second end card with format name and episode number, cut so the video loops into its opening.
- Cover frames carry format name and episode title.

**Sound**
- Original audio first. Music under montage only, never under speech.
- Music only from TikTok's Commercial Music Library or Lagos artists with written permission.
- A Tour Studios sound ident closes every video (not composed yet).

**Picture**
- Reframe 16:9 shot by shot, on purpose. Handheld, observational, honest skin tones.
- No stock footage, no AI faces or voices.

**Search and caption text**
- The search phrase goes in three places: first line of the caption, the on-screen title, and said aloud early.
- Three to five specific hashtags. Never #fyp. Tag the venue location.

**Never**
- Dance trends, lip-syncs, chart-song memes, rage bait, laughing at people on camera.
- TikTok-watermarked files on Instagram or Instagram files on TikTok. Cut from clean masters.
- A paid video without the Paid partnership label. No more than one paid video in five.
- Posting to fill a slot.

**Dates**
- Launch Monday 16 November 2026 with eight videos banked: three Tour With… recuts, three At the Door, one This Weekend, one spare.
- Daily from 14 December to 4 January.

| Kotn, "Third Culture Kid" | 64 s | 25 | One seated interview, same questions every episode | The model for a repeatable Tour With… or profile. Opens on the question "What's your name and where are you from?". One static frame, jump cuts on every answer, roughly one cut every 2.5 s. | Refined Ambition, "Do what you love" | 20 s | 8 | Podcast quote with animated typography over film clips | Large kinetic type, key words in colour, b-roll changes on the beat of the sentence. One idea, 20 seconds, 3.4 million views. |

Common to all five:
- The first frame is a face or a claim, never a logo.
- Every spoken word is on screen.
- One idea per video.
- Original voice audio carries it; music is absent or far underneath.
- The format is recognisable in one second and identical every episode.

## 3. What Tayo's Guide already does right

Tayo's Guide to Lagos: 3:04, vertical, about 132 cuts (one every 1.4 s).

- **Cold open on the best line.** It starts mid-sentence on "you have to experience a Lagos rave", before the name and title. This is the PDF's first-two-seconds rule already in practice.
- **B-roll proves every claim.** FOMO, the Thai food, the rave, Mako, the WBF store and the museum each appear as he names them.
- **One strong interview frame.** White tee and red trousers against the red wall of Fantasy Ikoyi. Instantly recognisable as the same person each time the cut returns.
- **Chapter title cards.** "One day. Your Lagos. Where to?" in the red display lettering marks a new section.
- **Bug in the top corners** on every shot, and small white sentence-case subtitles low in frame.
- **Personality over information.** The Mako confession and the NYSC warning are the most shareable moments and neither is a recommendation.

What changes for TikTok:
- 3:04 is over the 60–120 s ceiling. It becomes one main cut plus short single-idea cuts.
- The subtitles are small for a phone and sit low; they need to move up and grow.
- The title card arrives at about 4 seconds; on TikTok the title is on screen from frame one.
- It needs the search phrase said or shown early ("things to do in Lagos").

## 5. Open conflicts

1. **Caption style.** The PDF bans word-by-word pop-up captions and asks for calm broadcast subtitles. Three of the five references use the opposite: Kotn (one to three words, all caps), Refined Ambition and Diary of a CEO (large animated words). SubwayTakes and Jessica Park match the PDF.
2. **Guide to Lagos has no slot.** The PDF's four formats do not include it, yet the best film is a Guide. The plan above treats it as Tour With….
3. **Clean masters.** The published exports have subtitles, bugs and title cards burned in. Proper recuts need the clean masters or project files from the Video Editing folder.
4. **Still to be supplied or decided (PDF section 12):** the house sans typeface file, the channel bug artwork, the TikTok handle, the sound ident.

## 8. Hooks (three examples supplied by Folu, 12 Nov 2026)

The examples are hook-formula videos (Julia Broome's weekly hook schedule, and two "10 hooks that work" lists). What they teach, translated for Tour Studios, where the subject speaks and we do not:

| Hook type | What it is | How we use it |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-sentence | Start as if the conversation is already going | Cold-open on the subject mid-thought ("…I'm not proud to say this") |
| Bold claim | A statement people want to argue with | Open on the subject's biggest claim ("We are the new Lagos") |
| Reverse psychology | Tell people not to do or look | On-screen line that dares ("Ask this Lagos DJ for country music") |
| Probing question | An observation plus a real question | On-screen question the video answers ("Why is this Lagos building painted red?") |
| Vulnerability | Something admitted | Open on the admission ("I'm always tired", "I just got leaves") |
| Headline as hook | The typography is the hook | Larger headline held for the first 3.5 seconds |

Phrase templates from the examples that fit our voice: "Watch this before you…", "This is your sign to…", "This might surprise you", "Nobody talks about…", "You're doing … wrong".
Ones to avoid because they are creator-speak, not ours: "Stop scrolling", "I almost didn't post this", "You need to hear this".

Structure of a hook-first cut: the punchline first (1 to 3 seconds), then the set-up, then the punchline again in context as the pay-off.
The on-screen hook must be true to what the person says; never promise something the clip does not deliver.

## 9. Feedback on the first raw-footage cuts (Folu, 12 Nov 2026)

Folu's words: "the outro is too emotionless use our normal video outro and let the cuts make sense give the videos more soul".

What changed in response:
- **Outro.** No blurred "Follow Tour Studios" card. End the way the finished films do: the subject's own sign-off line ("you've just been on Tour with me") with the "Plan your Lagos Experience with Tour" graphic fading in over live footage. The graphic was recovered from Tayo's Guide; a clean file from the editor would be better.
- **Order.** Tell the story in the order it was said. Do not open on the punchline and then replay it (my reading of "let the cuts make sense").
- **Soul.** Cut away to b-roll of what the person is talking about, and keep the human moments (laughs, reactions) instead of trimming to the last word.
- Still no music: no licensed track has been supplied.
