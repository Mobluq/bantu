# First TikTok cuts — 11 November 2026

Nine draft cuts made from two finished exports: Tayo's Guide to Lagos (Instagram export) and Discover Leduum (Drive file).
All are 1080×1920, 25 fps, with a title on screen from the first frame in Archivo, the sans used on tourstudio.tv.

These are drafts, not masters:
- The source films already had subtitles, the Tour bug and title cards burned in, so those are still visible. No new subtitles were added on top.
- Music in the source jumps at some cut points because the bed could not be separated from the voice.
- No end card or sound ident yet; both need brand files.
